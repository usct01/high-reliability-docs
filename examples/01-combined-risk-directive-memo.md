MEMORANDUM TO ALL PORTFOLIO MANAGERS AND QUANTITATIVE TRADING DESKS

SUBJECT:  MANDATORY PORTFOLIO CONCENTRATION LIMITS AND DRAWDOWN CONTROLS

1.  Purpose.  This directive sets issuer exposure caps and drawdown circuit breakers for all equity funds, effective 01 December 2026.

2.  Mandatory Risk Requirements.  All active equity portfolios SHALL satisfy the following verifiable thresholds:

    a.  Single-Issuer Concentration (REQ-RSK-001).  Each portfolio SHALL cap individual equity issuer exposure at 5.0 percent of Net Asset Value (NAV) at market close, verified via daily pre-settlement compliance scans.

    b.  Sector Weight Cap (REQ-RSK-002).  Each portfolio SHALL restrict aggregate Global Industry Classification Standard (GICS) sector allocation to 25.0 percent of NAV, verified via daily risk attribution reports.

    c.  Intraday Drawdown Circuit Breaker (REQ-RSK-003).  The Order Management System (OMS) SHALL halt new buy orders for any strategy whose intraday mark-to-market loss reaches 2.5 percent of starting NAV, verified via automated staging simulations.

3.  Escalation.  Submit written exception requests to the Investment Risk Committee at least 48 hours before trade execution.
