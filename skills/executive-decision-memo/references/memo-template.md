# Standard Executive Memorandum Layout

```text
[ORGANIZATION / DIVISION HEADER]
[STREET ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE POSTAL CODE]

[DEPARTMENT CODE] (e.g., ENG-OPS-01)                  [DD Month YYYY]

MEMORANDUM TO [Recipient Team / Role / Department]
*(OR: MEMORANDUM ROUTED VIA [Approver] TO [Final Recipient])*

SUBJECT:  [CONCISE SUBJECT IN ALL CAPS]

1.  Purpose.  [State the BLUF clearly in 1–2 sentences.]

2.  Background.  [Provide necessary context, policy references, or business drivers.]

    a.  [First sub-point, indented 4 spaces.]

    b.  [Second sub-point. Ensure at least two sub-points exist.]

3.  Point of Contact.  The undersigned is the point of contact for this memorandum at [Phone] or [Email].


Attachments                             FIRST M. LAST
1. [Attachment Title]                   Title / Role
                                        Department / Division
```
